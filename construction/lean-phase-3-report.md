## Judgments and limitations

Live checks found and repaired hidden metrics when GM was disabled, duplicated
Overview loop cards, missing displayed GM recommendations, and an SSE shutdown
deadlock. No acceptance gate was weakened. Two private GM fixture configurations
failed before a worker started; both records remain. The corrected launchd proof
made one model call, retained an evidence-linked concern and stayed proposal-only.

Linux scheduler behavior is deterministically tested, not claimed as live Linux
proof. No merged PR or deployed feature exists in these local fixtures, so those
ratios stay unavailable. No broad savings claim follows from this sample; failed
validation attempts erased most of the initial successful-path token advantage.
Construction overhead and measurement gaps are recorded in `lean-efficiency.md`.
Creator is unchanged. Test scheduler jobs were unloaded after proof; this is not
an appointment or activation of a production GM. No push or merge is included.
