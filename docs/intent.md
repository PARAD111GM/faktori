# Faktori intent

## Promise

An owner can point a coding agent at this repository, describe a product, and
receive a working, self-hosted software factory configured to the owner’s
resources, provider choice, budget, and approval boundaries.

## V1 boundaries

V1 supports one local factory host, multiple products and pods where justified,
GitHub by default, optional Jira integration, and Codex, Claude Code, or
Cursor through their unmodified CLIs. It includes a local Console and a
durable coordinator. It does not include a hosted service, billing,
marketplace, analytics, 3D visualization, or distributed workers.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. Version-controlled intent, approved configuration, and specifications are
   authoritative; dashboards are projections.
2. Workers are bounded and disposable. Publication is performed by a trusted
   coordinator with independently checked authority.
3. A green process, PR, CI run, or deployment is evidence of that event only;
   acceptance requires evidence tied to the requested scope.
4. Authentication remains in the provider-owned flow and credential store.
   Faktori never exports or stores provider session tokens.
5. Human approval, merge, production-release, spending, and authority changes
   remain explicit and revocable.
6. Recovery is a first-class behavior: intended external operations and their
   observed results are durable and reconciled after a crash.

## Success boundary

The first meaningful proof is one small ticket moving from approved intent to
reviewable change, verified tests, and release evidence on a real registered
product repository. Similarity to any other factory is not an acceptance
criterion.
