# v0.2.15 — native artifact adoption

Native local delivery can enter the existing observation, adoption and next
discovery cycle. The adapter seals an iOS app directory or Android APK with
bounded streaming reads, and binds accepted requirements/design, source,
verification, application/runtime identity and the shared backend contract.
The independently approved observer checks identity before and after the full
native journey. Changed artifacts or evidence prevent adoption and replay.

This profile qualifies local simulator/emulator delivery only. It cannot claim
physical-device qualification, production signing, store distribution or
factory publication. Requirements discovery and the subsequent owner feature
cycle retain their existing authority and revision checks.

Typecheck/build, the thirteen-stage source journey, independent review and the
clean installed-package feature journeys passed for this increment. The new
sealer also read the retained actual qualified iOS app and Android APK. Artifact
seals are distinct from device observation and accepted native-product adoption.
The human
qualification packet prepares the remaining discovery holdout, calibration and
sustained-outcome gates without supplying human labels or elapsed observations.
Native-goal automation remains unqualified after the v0.2.14 real-runtime
activation finding; attended bounded execution remains the supported route.

Update through the owner-controlled package update and restart. Existing
managed and externally hosted profiles retain their own qualification scope.
