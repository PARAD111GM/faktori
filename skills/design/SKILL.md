# Design

Inputs are the plan, context packet, approved references, constraints, and dependency contracts. Output interfaces, data/interaction decisions, alternatives, and risk notes at compact, normal, or high-risk scale proportional to change. Keep language/framework choices local to implementation; preserve ancestor constraints. Evidence is the approved design revision and review/authority record. Escalate unapproved references, incompatible contracts, security/data risk, or design changes that rewrite scope.
