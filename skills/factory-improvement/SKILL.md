# Factory improvement

Inputs are an observed problem, baseline evidence, affected factory contracts, and approval authority. Output a shaped improvement node, lifecycle package, measured change, and rollback/recovery note. Use compact for documentation/repair, normal for behavior changes, and high-risk for coordinator, isolation, credentials, or migration changes. Completion evidence compares before/after observations at exact revisions. Escalate when evidence is anecdotal, contracts conflict, or improvement would widen authority or bypass provider-owned authentication.
