# Maintain

Inputs are release revision/environment, health signals, incidents, ownership, and recovery plan. Output a maintenance record, follow-up nodes, and observed recovery or drift evidence. Use compact for routine low-risk upkeep, normal for planned maintenance, and high-risk for incidents or data-impacting recovery. Preserve hierarchy and authority. Escalate unhealthy state, missing owner, drift, irreversible remediation, or absent recovery receipts.
