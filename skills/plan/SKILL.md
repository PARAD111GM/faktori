# Plan

Inputs are the accepted objective, criteria, context packet, hierarchy node, dependencies, budget, and authority. Output a compact intent for small work, a normal specification/implementation plan, or separate high-risk plan and dependency breakdown. Record all six lifecycle outcomes, including explicit reasons for inapplicable stages. Completion evidence is a revision-bound plan with commands, risks, estimates, and approval. Escalate scope changes, missing contracts, or authority/budget uncertainty.
